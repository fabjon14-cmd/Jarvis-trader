consequence of the 5-position cap and 5%-of-buying-power sizing rule. The
same low-invested-fraction posture that has repeatedly capped the account's
upside in rallying weeks (e.g., the 11-13 week's underperformance during
SPY's rebound) nearly fully cushioned it from this week's broad decline.
