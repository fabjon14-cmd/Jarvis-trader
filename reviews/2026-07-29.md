## Rule adherence

**Violation found: AAPL buy placed inside the 48h pre-earnings blackout.**
The 2026-07-29 Trading Session journal entry states, in its own reasoning for
both AAPL and MSFT: *"AAPL and MSFT both report earnings within the next ~48
hours."* CLAUDE.md's rule is explicit: *"Do not open a new position within
48h of a symbol's earnings report — event risk without a directional edge is
a hold, not a bet. Existing positions are a separate call ... this only
blocks new buys."* The AAPL order was a **new** position (no prior AAPL
holding), opened with earnings already flagged as imminent (~48h out) in the
same entry. The journal's justification — "it's a disclosed, symmetric risk
rather than a reason to avoid entirely" — is exactly the kind of
risk/reward reasoning the rule was written to override; the rule doesn't
carve out an exception for small, well-sized, or "symmetric" bets, it's a
flat hold. This should not have been placed. Sizing itself was fine (5
shares / ~$1,705 notional, well under the $2,000 unattended cap and 10%
buying-power limit), and it was correctly a limit order — the violation is
specifically the earnings-window timing, not sizing or order type.
